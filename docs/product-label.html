Choose the shot around the surface

  1. Pick a 3–5 second section from your existing clip for the first editing test. This is an edit length, not a generation-request setting.
  2. For a flat panel, track the surface and attach the artwork using the perspective/corner-pin controls your editor provides. Place the art on the correct plane.
  3. Mask anything that passes in front, such as a hand or an opening flap. Match softness, motion blur and lighting so the label moves with the shot.
  4. Inspect moving frames at normal speed and step through difficult moments. A logo overlay cannot repair a product that changes shape or a shot with no usable tracking surface.

A simple four-corner pin fits a planar surface. Curved or bending surfaces need additional mapping or deformation work; use your existing editor's appropriate tools or choose a simpler shot.
