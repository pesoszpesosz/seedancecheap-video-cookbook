Drop-Off

A parcel hits a dead end. Then the floor moves.

Stage the reveal

Start with the parcel already moving. Give it one obstacle the viewer can read, then use the camera to show what the obstacle was hiding.

Replay the three beats (spoilers)

Each replay starts just before its cue. Try the landing muted, then with sound.

  1. 0.58 s

    Impact. A sharp knock and a short recoil sell the hit against the striped stop.

  2. 2.68 s

    Drop. A motor starts before the opening is clear. Then the camera follows the parcel down.

  3. 3.42 s

    Landing. A soft thud puts the parcel in the tray. A two-note chime starts at 4.38 s as the green panel confirms the delivery.
