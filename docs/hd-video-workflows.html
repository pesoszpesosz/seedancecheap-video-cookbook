Try the HD remix setup.

You can inspect the workflow and review a quote before starting a generation.

  1. Open Genjutsu and choose a library reference or upload a clip you are authorized to use.
  2. Choose Restyle. Add your style image, or open Output settings and use Use source frame as reference image.
  3. In Output settings, select 720p, 15s and the aspect ratio you need. Our checked quote used Landscape 16:9.
  4. Choose the audio behavior, then Review quote. Our earlier checked combination returned 1 credit; it is not a current offer. Review the available route and its complete price. The separate start button begins the paid request.
