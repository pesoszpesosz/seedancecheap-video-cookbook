The Catch

The cable snaps. A single steel pin has to catch the load.

Three frames that sell the catch

  1. The cable has separated. The shaft ribs give the falling cage a fixed reference.
  2. The pin is already across the cage foot’s path. You can see the gap before the hit.
  3. The cage foot meets the pin, the fall stops and the latch light comes on. Play from just before the catch to hear the impact.

When you review an action take, look for a visible gap before impact and a readable point of contact after it.
